New trace findings:
- First H2 ellipse UV is (pi, 3.10453160765): the inverse picked the negative-radial
  half of a full ellipse, instead of the positive sheet near meridian .037061.
- Before the interior pole, major reaches 7.85398163397; at the pole it jumps to
  6.26481765400, then 3.14159265359. The meridian reaches pi/2.
- ProjectedCurve projects to (height, abs(radial)), so a full ellipse double-covers
  the same physical surface. Default presearch can choose the other sheet through
  roundoff. No-hint projection therefore changes orientation on this representation.
- RevolutedCurve::search_parameter preserves angular hints only at front/back
  poles, not interior poles. It normalizes tiny radial vectors at an interior pole.
- PolyBoundaryPiece unwraps each periodic coordinate by the shortest difference.
  A 270-degree pole transition can choose the complementary 90-degree jump.
- PolyBoundary::new adds a full parameter-domain boundary if no positive outer
  loop was found. On a double-covered ellipse this can add the wrong whole domain.
- RevolutedCurve::normal also only special-cases endpoint poles.
