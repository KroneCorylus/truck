What was verified

  • All 80 constructed replay outputs preserve the input, have consistent geometry and closed meshes, and export as native-valid single solids.
  • 78 satisfy the combined limited comparisons, including sampled distances of at most 0.001 in both directions. This is sampled evidence, not proof of complete equivalence.
  • The torus batch’s five-crate validation recorded 893 passes, one previously reproduced PCurve precision failure and five existing ignored tests.
  • The final crash-fix checkpoint passed 349 shape-operations tests, with no failures and one existing ignored test. All-target Clippy and the action benchmark smoke check passed.
  • New seam-selection and G9 fixture regressions reproduce the parent failure. No existing assertions were weakened or removed.

The earlier torus-import benchmarks recorded an extra 0.4–0.7 ms on E5/E9 because validation now completes checks that previously exited early. That cost is documented; no speedup is claimed.

What remains

The last full replay also recorded 37 construction failures, 21 import failures, 11 inconsistent inputs, eight unhandled requests, two timeouts, one panic and one incomplete capture.

The panic is historical: it is G9’s crash from the full run. The committed fix passes its targeted checks and now reports unsupported imported geometry. A new full replay is the next validation step.

  • H2/H4 timeouts: H2 constructs, then tessellation expands an ellipsoid’s parameter region incorrectly near a pole. The investigation is saved; no fix is claimed.
  • Geometry gaps: G9 surface-curve conversion, E9 torus trimming, joins across multiple curved faces, unequal radii, and more general corner collapse and collision cases.
  • Comparison gaps: G1 and tolerance-grid A1 still differ from the native result.
  • Broader coverage: advanced chamfers, variable-radius behavior, open contours and 2D/face-driven cases still need work. Missing fixtures prevent execution of 701 catalog entries, including the 279 dedicated chamfer cases.

The catalog contains 50 capability families plus one reference topic, covering 1,026 entries from 795 files. There is no reliable overall completion percentage yet.
