Work completed

  • Per-edge radii: try_fillet_solid_edges_with_radii exposes the existing straight-edge solver with one radius per selected edge. It supports planar edges and axial cylindrical lips, retains original-to-final face history, and leaves the input unchanged on failure.
  • Predictable selection: construction order is independent of selection order. Equal radii keep the existing solid-fillet behavior, including its corner support. Empty selections retain the original solid.
  • Faithful replay: every captured radius is passed to the solver. Repeated physical selections with the same radius are deduplicated; conflicting radii and invalid assignments are rejected.
  • Geometry proof: three parent-failing replay regressions and four public API regressions cover independent volumes, rolling radii and normals, scale/rotation/translation, selection permutations, complete history, mesh closure, STEP, overlapping rounds and immutable failure.

Unequal-radius selections currently require separate straight edges without shared vertices. General unequal-radius corners and unequal curved contours remain unsupported. The operation does not add global collision detection.
