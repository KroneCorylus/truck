Work done

The existing straight-edge solver can walk past that edge while retaining the support faces, but was not tried after this rejection. Equal and unequal isolated-edge chamfers now reach that solver. Its boundary and geometry checks stay in force; if it rejects the input, the original diagnostic is retained.
