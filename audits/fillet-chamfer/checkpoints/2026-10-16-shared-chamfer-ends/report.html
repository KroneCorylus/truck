Work done

A curved rim can have a cap bounded by only an arc and a diameter, with both chamfer ends reaching the same terminal face. Assembly previously tried to close that face using just one end’s bridge. It now collects both bridges before rebuilding the face and requires each bridge to close exactly one gap.

Two new regressions fail on the parent and pass after the fix. Six variants cover equal and unequal setbacks, scaling, rigid transforms and reversed selection. They check analytic material and cone geometry, outward normals, unchanged input, topology, closed meshes and STEP through the shared harnesses.

Three fresh native controls also replay as valid single-solid STEP outputs. Their largest sampled boundary difference is below 7 × 10−9, and the largest analytic STEP volume difference is below 2.14 × 10−8. Contact fitting, distance ordering and public APIs are unchanged.

Earlier in this continuation, G1’s independent geometry checks established sampled native reference error while retaining the existing corpus verdict.
