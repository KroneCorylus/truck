DERIVATION
Take D=(0,-1,0), F=(D.X)D, div F=1. The cap planes, closing wall and cap/wall
cylinder have normals perpendicular D and hence ZERO flux. The complete volume
is retained extrusion + cap/extrusion canal + selected ruling cylinder +
selected sphere. The retained extrusion integral is split at remote Q.
This is also the section-area formula integral xi d eta integrated over depth.

Write L=sqrt(26+s²), K=sqrt(1+s²). Oriented E normal N=(5,s,-1)/L.
Cap-plane radial direction V=(0,s,-1)/K. Cap/E rolling center is
 C=(200-r,1000s-rs/L,500s²+1000-5r+26r/L).
Cap/W centerline has x=200-r,z=1500-5r-rsqrt26. Its radial direction is +z.
Canal X=C+r[n cos(phi)+V sin(phi)], n=(1,0,0), phi=q alpha(s),
alpha(s)=acos(5/L). Seam with cap/W cylinder is
 g(s,q)=500(s²-1)+r(26/L+sqrt26)-r sin(q alpha)(1+1/K)=0.
Remote root lies -1<s<0. Selected stationary contact ss=-root(g(s,0));
remote extrusion contact sq=root(g(s,1)). For these tested radii it is always
E-first. E contact angle is small (<.28 rad) while W contact is >2.94 rad.
No basis continuation outside [-1,1] is used.

Retained E spans s in[-1,ss]. For s>=sq its upper extrusion parameter is
v=(200-r+5r/L)/200. For remote s<=sq it is clipped by cap/W cylinder:
 Kc=500(s²-1)+r sqrt26,
 x=200-r+[-5Kc+sqrt(26r²-Kc²)]/26; v=x/200.
This selects the cylinder branch continuous from Q to R, not the other root.
The substitution s=-1+(sq+1)t² removes the square-root endpoint singularity.
E flux integrand is 2e8*s²*v, from B_s cross A and F(B+vA).

Canal: for each q in[0,1], integrate s from remote root(g(s,q)) to ss.
Outward vector area is X_q cross X_s. alpha'(s) cancels in this cross product.
Selected longitudinal cylinder: center C(ss)+tA, normal arc from N(ss) to m,
m=(-5,0,1)/sqrt26. Integrate t analytically from the unselected bottom cap
 tmin=(-200+r-r*n.R)/(n.A) to 0, then quadrature over the normal arc.
Selected sphere: center C(ss), normals in minor spherical triangle n,N(ss),m.
These sphere/cylinder/canal boundaries share their exact analytic arcs.

INDEPENDENT SPHERE CROSS-CHECK
Numerically integrate R=b/|b|, b=(1-u)(1-v)n+u*m+(1-u)v*N, with Jacobian
abs(det(n,m,N))*(1-u)/|b|³. Low orders converge slowly because n,m nearly oppose.
Separately compute exact moments of the oriented spherical triangle [n,N,m].
For directed edge a->b, theta=acos(a.b), e=(a cross b)/sin(theta):
 M1=integral R dOmega=.5 sum(theta*e).
 Omega=2 atan2(det(n,N,m),1+n.N+N.m+m.n).
 M2=integral(D.R)²dOmega=Omega/3+
    sum[(D.e)*(D.(a+b))*tan(theta/2)]/3.
The second identity follows from spherical Laplacian Delta[(D.R)²]=2-6(D.R)²
and outward great-circle conormal -e. Sphere flux is r²(D.C)D.M1+r³M2.
The final oracle uses these moments, retaining the numerical integral as an
independent check. At r10, numerical256 vs moments differs <5e-9 captured³;
orders512/1024 agree to floating precision. No native value enters this check.
