# Recognizing translated procedural cylinders

The original R10 first-round surface is stored as a procedural fillet, although its geometric support is a cylinder. Analytic recognition now proves a translated circular section from its representation: two explicit plane supports, valid clamped contact data, parallel monotone contact control polygons with constant separation, and constant tangent/weight coefficients. The homogeneous cubic section must reduce to a rational quadratic; the existing circular-row control/weight checks then establish the cylinder. A read-only section-coefficient accessor provides the stored data. No surface is fitted or replaced, and no sample-only classifier is added. The existing NURBS circular-row arithmetic is shared without changing its checks.

This lets the previously committed collapsed-contact constructor handle the original procedural R10 fixture and newly generated procedural first rounds. The helper still rejects larger-radius folds and unsupported geometry; this change does not add a different corner algorithm. Variable coefficients, noncircular sections, nonmonotone contact polygons, zero-range knots, mismatched coefficient arrays and anisotropic transforms remain unsupported. Unordered knots already fail deserialization. Recognition is conservative and does not cover every procedural cylinder or every parameterization.
