# Coincident cylindrical terminal fans

Concave planar chamfers now preserve internal seams when their terminal fan lies on coincident, co-oriented cylindrical faces. The former planar-only cap patch is generalized to a cylinder: its new edge is the exact plane/cylinder section, its other edges are the removed portions of the original boundary, and its surface preserves the existing cylinder. Existing faces stay in index order. Coincidence requires matching radius/axis/centerline and local outward normal. Empty consumed-neighbor paths and a nonempty terminal fan remain required. Patch samples must stay in the cylinder's visible half and form a valid polygon in its tangent-plane projection. Fillets and noncoincident terminal fans retain their prior route.
